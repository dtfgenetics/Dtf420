# Trichomes & Resin evidence notes

- Use Cannabis-specific trichome studies for gland morphology, developmental state, tissue specialization, and sampling claims.
- The 2020 Plant Journal work supports a developmental relationship between sessile-like premature stalked floral glands and mature stalked glands; do not claim that every sessile gland on every organ must become stalked.
- The 2026 head-versus-stalk multi-omics study supports distinct tissue programs. Describe stalk transport, precursor-supply, sugar, hormone-signaling, and vascular-associated signatures cautiously; do not depict a fully proven vascular conduit or invent flux rates.
- The 2024 biofactory review supports secretory-disk/cuticle/storage-cavity cell biology. Keep the storage cavity extracellular and distinguish it from secretory cells.
- Several intracellular-to-extracellular metabolite transport steps remain incompletely resolved. Mark uncertain steps as research questions rather than filling gaps with a definitive pathway.
- Clear/cloudy/amber appearance is an optical observation, not a direct cannabinoid assay.
- Use representative flower-bract samples and standardized lighting, focus, magnification, canopy position, and repeated observations. Sugar-leaf glands should not automatically substitute for bract glands.
- Gland aging, dehiscence, collapse, detachment, handling damage, genotype, and tissue age can all affect microscope appearance; one gland cannot represent the whole plant.
