# Nodes & Branching source notes

The expansion uses the following evidence hierarchy:

- **Cannabis morphology:** Morphological Characterization of Cannabis sativa L. Throughout Its Complete Life Cycle (Plants, 2023) for axillary buds, stipules, reproductive node structures, and the documented opposite-to-alternate phyllotaxy transition in the studied genotype.
- **Cannabis architecture manipulation:** Shape Matters: Plant Architecture Affects Chemical Uniformity in Large-Size Medical Cannabis Plants (Plants, 2021) for pruning, branch-removal, canopy-light, height, and architectural-plasticity observations in large drug-type plants.
- **Cannabis pruning:** Impact of Harvest Time and Pruning Technique on Total CBD Concentration and Yield of Medicinal Cannabis (Plants, 2022) for apical-cut and side-shoot-removal responses in one chemotype-III genotype.
- **General shoot-branching physiology:** Lessons from a century of apical dominance research (Journal of Experimental Botany, 2023) and Light Regulation of Axillary Bud Outgrowth Along Plant Axes (Frontiers in Plant Science, 2019) for auxin, cytokinin, strigolactone, sugar, light, and bud-outgrowth models.
- **General biomechanics:** Physiological and Physical Strategies to Minimize Damage at the Branch–Stem Junction of Trees (Plants, 2023) for attachment geometry and stress-transfer concepts only; these are not Cannabis-specific strength measurements.
