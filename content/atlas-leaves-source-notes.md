# Leaves expansion source notes

## Evidence hierarchy

- **Cannabis leaf anatomy:** the 2022 pharmacognostic study provides Cannabis-specific transverse-section evidence for epidermis, palisade and spongy mesophyll, vascular/support tissues, trichomes, and cystolith-bearing structures. It is used for anatomy, not universal physiological thresholds.
- **Cannabis leaf morphogenesis:** the 2023 complete-life-cycle White Widow study documents changing leaflet complexity and leaf area across development. Its node-by-node sequence is kept explicitly genotype- and condition-specific.
- **Leaf age and photosynthetic capacity:** the 2020 hemp crown study repeatedly measured leaves after full expansion and found declining photosynthetic capacity with age even while visual greenness changed much less. Its fitted timing is not converted into a universal defoliation schedule.
- **Light and temporal photosynthesis:** the 2021 indoor Cannabis study shows that leaf light-response behavior changes with localized PPFD and flowering time; leaf photosynthesis is not treated as a stand-alone yield predictor.
- **Stomatal traits:** the 2019 Cannabis polyploid study demonstrates context-dependent stomatal size/density differences. Those values are examples tied to ploidy and study conditions rather than species-wide reference ranges.
- **Genotype-aware leaf form and gas exchange:** the 2022 medical-Cannabis breeding-population study supports treating leaflet form and measured photosynthetic traits as population/genotype-dependent observations.
